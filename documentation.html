Official Product Specification & Architecture

NutriPulse AI: End-to-End System & Workflow Documentation

Comprehensive technical and user flow specification covering authentication, multi-tenant partner isolation, clinical nutrition calculations, Indian meal planning, AI food scanning, and dynamic WhatsApp AI coach deep-linking.

1. Executive Summary & Core Loop

NutriPulse AI is an adaptive fitness and nutrition platform built to satisfy the uploaded FitLife AI specification. Instead of static diet plans, the system operates on an active feedback loop:

Core Loop: Member Baseline → Mifflin-St Jeor TDEE → Daily Meal Generation → Food & Water Logging → AI Meal Swaps → Weekly Behavioral Adjustment → WhatsApp AI Accountability.

2. Complete User Flows (From Login to Feature Execution)

Flow 1: Partner & Gym Owner Registration (Admin Gateway)

  • Access: Admin clicks 🔐 Partner Admin Gateway in the footer.
  • Information Stored: Firm Name, Brand Logo, WhatsApp Phone Number, Physical Address, City, Admin Email, Generated Password, Custom AI Bot Name, and WhatsApp Welcome Message.
  • Database Persistence: Saved directly to the SQLite admins table.
  • Session Security: Issues an isolated admin session token (ADM_TOKEN_*).

Flow 2: Member (Customer) 3-Step Guided Registration

  • Step 1 (Account & Hub): Full Name, Email, Password, and selects their preferred Partner Fitness Club.
  • Step 2 (Body Baseline): Age, Biological Sex, Height (cm), Weight (kg), Target Weight (kg), Waist (cm), Activity Level, Weekly Workouts.
  • Step 3 (Diet & Safety Screening): Primary Goal (Fat-loss, Muscle-gain, Maintenance), Diet Type (Veg, Non-veg, Jain, Vegan), Regional Cuisine (North/South Indian), Allergies, and Clinical Screening flags (Diabetes, BP, Thyroid).

Flow 3: Clinical Nutrition Calculation Layer

Calculates individualized energy and macro targets based on clinical formulas:

  • BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor):
    Male: (10 × weight) + (6.25 × height) - (5 × age) + 5
    Female: (10 × weight) + (6.25 × height) - (5 × age) - 161
  • TDEE: Multiplies BMR by activity factor (1.2 Desk Job to 1.725 Athlete).
  • Calorie Budget: TDEE - 450 kcal for Fat-loss (minimum 1,300 kcal) or TDEE + 350 kcal for Hypertrophy.
  • Protein Target: 1.8g to 2.0g per kg of bodyweight.
  • Hydration Target: 35 ml per kg of bodyweight.

Flow 4: Today's Dashboard & Net Calorie Math

Features an animated circular Calorie Budget Wheel:

Net Calorie Equation:
Net Calories = Calories Consumed - Calories Burned
When a member logs a 45-minute workout, their net calorie allowance expands safely!

Flow 5: Indian Meal Plan & AI Swap Engine

Provides 5 daily meal slots featuring standardized Indian food items (Moong Dal Chilla, Idli Sambar, Ragi Dosa, Dal Tadka with Phulkas, Paneer Bhurji, Curd Rice, Sprouts, Makhana). Clicking "AI Swap" queries dishes with equivalent calories (±100 kcal) so members never feel trapped in one menu.

Flow 6: AI Food Scanner (Photo Recognition)

Simulates camera photo scanning of Indian plates. Detects food components, displays an AI confidence rating (e.g. 94% High), provides portion adjustment sliders (0.5× to 2.0×), and logs directly to daily intake.

Flow 7: Workout & Calorie Burn Tracker

Members log exercise sessions (Strength Training, HIIT, Running, Cycling, Walking, Yoga). Calorie burn is calculated automatically and credited to the daily net calorie budget.

Flow 8: High-Protein Indian Recipe Guides

Step-by-step cooking guides for Moong Dal Chilla, Paneer Bhurji, Idli Sambar, Dal Tadka, and Sprouts Chaat, including ingredients with measures, prep time, cook time, and chef's nutrition tips.

Flow 9: Smart Weekly Grocery & Pantry Checklist

Generates an interactive shopping list categorized into Pulses & Staples, Fresh Proteins & Dairy, Whole Grains, and Produce with check-off checkboxes.

Flow 10: Clinical BMI & Target Timeline Calculator

Displays live BMI classification (Underweight, Normal, Overweight, Obese), resting BMR, and predicts the exact timeline (in weeks) to reach the target weight based on sustainable 0.5 kg/week progress.

Flow 11: Dynamic WhatsApp AI Agent Integration

  • Pulsing green launcher widget positioned at the bottom right.
  • Dynamically bound to the exact WhatsApp phone number registered by the member's fitness partner club.
  • One-click deep link: https://wa.me/{admin_phone}?text={user_context}.
  • In-app interactive AI coach answering questions on workouts, meal swaps, and restaurant options.
